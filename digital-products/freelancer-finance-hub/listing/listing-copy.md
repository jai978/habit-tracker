## Description

**Stop doing your freelance books in your head.**

Freelance Finance Hub is a Google Sheets / Excel workbook that turns invoicing, income tracking, and tax planning into something that runs itself. Add a client once. Log an invoice. Everything else — invoice numbering, tax math, totals, overdue flags, monthly income, and how much to set aside for tax this quarter — updates automatically.

**What's included (6 connected tabs):**
- **Setup** — your business name, currency, tax/GST rate, and what % of income to set aside for tax. Set once, everything else reads from here.
- **Clients** — add a client once, then pick them from a dropdown on every invoice.
- **Invoice Log** — one row per invoice. Invoice numbers, tax, and totals calculate live. Status (Paid / Unpaid / Overdue) is color-coded automatically.
- **Invoice (Print)** — pick an invoice number and a clean, professional invoice fills in automatically, ready to save as PDF and send.
- **Dashboard** — total invoiced, total paid, outstanding, overdue, a paid-income-by-month chart, and — the part every freelancer avoids — exactly how much to set aside for tax this quarter.

**Why this one's different:**
Most invoice trackers you'll find are static — you're still doing the tax math yourself. This one calculates it for you, live, based on a set-aside percentage you control. No formulas to build. No Notion account required. No subscription.

**Works with:**
Google Sheets (File → Import → Upload) and Microsoft Excel — no add-ons, plugins, or paid tools needed.

**You'll get:**
One .xlsx file, instant download, with demo data included so you can see it working before you clear it and add your own.

## FAQ (for listing page)
**Do I need Excel to use this?** No — it works in the free Google Sheets too.
**Does it handle sales tax / GST?** Yes — set your rate once in Setup and every invoice applies it automatically.
**Can I add more than 3 clients / 8 invoices?** Yes — the formulas are already pre-filled down 300 rows.
**Is this financial or tax advice?** No — it's a tracking and estimation tool. Always confirm final tax obligations with a qualified accountant for your country.
